# Debugging Assessment

Give the developer a real, bounded defect. Do not reveal the root cause initially.

1. Reproduce the behavior and record the exact observation.
2. State a hypothesis before changing code.
3. Identify the smallest relevant execution path.
4. Use logs, debugger, tests, or inspection to isolate the cause.
5. Propose the smallest safe fix.
6. Add a regression test that fails before the fix and passes after it.
7. Explain why the defect occurred and why the fix is correct.
8. Identify one adjacent case that could still fail.

Record assistance A0–A4 and distinguish independent diagnosis from AI-supplied hypotheses or fixes.
