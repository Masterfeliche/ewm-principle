# Concept Explanation Assessment

Ask the developer to answer without solution-level AI help.

1. What problem does this concept solve?
2. Where does it appear in this repository?
3. Explain its execution / data flow in your own words.
4. What assumptions or invariants does it rely on?
5. What can go wrong? Give a concrete example.
6. What alternatives exist, and when might you choose them?
7. Which parts did you learn from AI or documentation?

**Evidence record:** concept, date, assistance A0–A4, observed accuracy, confidence, misconceptions, suggested level. An explanation alone rarely proves M3; pair it with implementation, debugging, or modification evidence.
