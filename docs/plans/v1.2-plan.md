# EMW v1.2 Implementation Plan

**Goal:** make the help limits the default instead of a per-task choice, keep the extra steps small, and give EMW evidence of its own.

**Why:** randomized trials found that AI help without limits harms learning, and that the harm goes away when the limits are built into the tool, not left to the learner (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025: unrestricted GPT −17% on exams, a tutor version with limits ≈ no harm). Developers who handed code-writing to AI kept least; those who asked for explanations kept most (Anthropic, Shen & Tamkin 2026). People overrate their own gains (METR 2025). EMW v1.1 describes the right modes but leaves the choice to the developer, so under deadline it falls back to delegation.

Order: 1 → 2 → 3, then 4 and 5 in parallel, then 6 and 7.

## Phase 1: task log
- Template: `.emw/templates/task-log.yaml`. Onboarding copies it to `.emw-project/task-log.yaml`.
- One entry per meaningful task: `id`, `date`, `summary`, `mode` (1–4), `assistance` (A0–A4), `skills` (ids from the skill ledger), optional `mode_reason`.
- Trivial quick-path tasks are not logged.
- `emw_check` validates fields, unique ids, dates, and that every skill id exists in `skill-ledger.yaml`. It warns when Mode 1 work is recorded as A4 (the human was meant to implement).

## Phase 2: mode policy
- Template: `.emw/templates/mode-policy.yaml`, copied to `.emw-project/mode-policy.yaml` and owned by the team lead.
- `default_mode_by_level` maps the weakest skill level in a task to the default mode (built-in: M0–M1 → 1, M2 → 2, M3–M4 → any).
- A task that uses less support than its default (a higher mode number) must carry `mode_reason`. So does Mode 4 on any skill below `mode4_min_level` (default M3).
- The agent reads the policy and proposes the default mode (`collaboration-modes.md`, Prompt 02). The human can override, with a reason.
- Without a policy file, `emw_check` applies the built-in defaults and warns.

## Phase 3: quick path
- First section of `AGENTS.md`: for trivial work, skip retrieval, ratings and ledgers; report Ran / Not run / Risks only.
- Trivial = under about 30 changed lines, no high-risk area, every touched skill at M3 or above (or no tracked skill).
- Anything else uses the full workflow.
